# Fuzzy Buddies Shape Club

A hands-on 3rd grade geometry game: shapes, angles, perimeter, area, points, lines, rays, symmetry, slides/flips/turns, and challenge levels.

**Play it:** https://raw.githack.com/Akbar300/pollys-polygon-playground/main/index.html

**GitHub Pages link:** https://akbar300.github.io/pollys-polygon-playground/ (this works once GitHub Pages is turned on for the `main` branch in the repo settings)

Open either link in Safari or Chrome. On iPhone or iPad, use Share and then Add to Home Screen to open it like an app.

## Combine & Split (added Nov 2026)
New activity for SOL 3.MG.4 f and g: push 2 or 3 shapes together and name the new polygon, name the pieces after a straight cut, and tap the cut that makes the pieces you want. Ported from the Shape Kingdom artifact; the polygon scenes tile exactly, and the Pick the Cut level computes the pieces from the geometry.
